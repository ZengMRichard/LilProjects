★ Recess ★

Run for It

A break between applications. The donkey runs down the Mall: jump the angry old congressmen, and mind the flying Stephen Millers. The farther you get, the more of Washington is repaired and the greener it grows.

Press Space or tap the picture to start.
  1. 1. Gridlockfrom the start

Space, the up arrow or a tap jumps. The down arrow or the Duck button ducks; hold it in the air to drop faster. Esc pauses. A figure flying high can be run under, one in the middle has to be ducked, and a low one has to be jumped. Your best run is kept in this browser. The game is satire: the congressmen are invented.

★ The Fine Print ★

How This Is Counted

Every figure on this page comes from the sources below, read on the date shown. Check the linked page before you rely on a date.

Your privacy

What this page collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no form that sends anything anywhere, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking script. The page sets no cookies of its own.

What stays in your browser

The postings and offices you star, and your best run in the game, are saved in this browser’s storage so they are still there on your next visit. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them.

What leaves your browser

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives that request and your IP address with it. A link to a posting takes you to that organization’s own site, under its own privacy policy. “+ Google Calendar” opens Google Calendar with the deadline filled in. The CSV file is made in your browser.

Who hosts it

Published as a Claude artifact, this page is served by Anthropic, and Anthropic’s privacy policy covers that hosting. A copy hosted anywhere else is covered by that host’s policy.

What the scraper gathers

Only pages anyone can open without logging in, read by a script that names itself and honors robots.txt when it crawls a site. Listings name members of Congress in their official role and, where an office prints one in its posting, the work address applications go to. Nothing about applicants is gathered. To have an entry corrected or removed, contact whoever runs this copy of the page.
